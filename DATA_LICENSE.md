# Data attribution and reuse

The derived `data/olist_orders.csv` is adapted from **Brazilian E-Commerce Public
Dataset by Olist**, released by Olist on Kaggle:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce

The source dataset is licensed **Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike
4.0 International**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

The derived CSV retains that license. Changes: order-item prices are summed per order;
item count and seller count are calculated; destination state is joined from customers;
selected order timestamps and final status are retained; columns are renamed. No
customer names, payment credentials, review text or precise geographic coordinates are
included. The app and derived data are used for noncommercial coursework. Describing a
business use case does not grant a commercial license to the original dataset.

`phone_trade_in.csv` is the instructor's simulated BANA409 Assignment 4 dataset:
https://bana409.harrywang.me/docs/assignments/assignment-4-linear-regression-sgd-and-random-forest

Its source is credited here; no separate ownership or public-domain claim is made.
